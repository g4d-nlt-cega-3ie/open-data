# Code of Conduct

Geo4Dev Open Data is a welcoming, collaborative space for researchers, practitioners, and
developers. We want everyone to be able to contribute and learn here.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in this community to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get oriented.
- Give and accept feedback graciously, focusing on what's best for the project and the
  research community.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, public or private.
- Discrimination or exclusionary jokes or language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Submitting content you do not have the right to share.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any related
communication — and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers through the
[Geo4Dev site](https://geo4dev.nltgis.ai/contact). Reports will be reviewed and handled
discreetly. Maintainers may remove content, or block contributors, that violates this code.

---

By participating, you agree to uphold this Code of Conduct so Geo4Dev stays a productive and
welcoming open-science community.
